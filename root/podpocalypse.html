Podpocalypse: Bottom of the Bowl

Premise
Pod-pocalypse is a couch party game set in the world of Jumbo and Pea-wee, two peas in a pod who face the imminent expiration of their world as they know it. With narrative and aesthetics inspired by post graduation fears and edamame salad, try to keep things together for as long as you can!

Role
I was co-creative director and gameplay designer, collaborating on initial concept, level design, and world-building. I was the sole developer for mechanics and systems, and lead technical artist for 3D environments, props, and VFX.

Tools
Procreate, Unity 6, Illustrator, Maya, Substance Painter
