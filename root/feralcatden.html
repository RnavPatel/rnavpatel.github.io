Nirvana Noir

Premise

A noir adventure beyond time and space. The city of cosmic beings is under threat in two parallel realities - one where the Big Bang was never fired, the other exploding in color and sin. It’s up to you to solve the mysteries of the Bigger Bang in this follow up to the award-winning Genesis Noir. Check out the Kickstarter↗.



Role & Scope
As a 3D Environment Artist and Prop Designer, I contributed to level and cutscene production by modeling, texturing, and set dressing assets that aligned with the game’s distinctive visual style.

Tools
Unreal Engine, Blender, C4D, Adobe CC

Designing for player exploration

The game narrative has a dense storyline that takes place simultaneously in two alternate universes, leaving a lot of opportunity for the player to explore as much of the world as possible. My role involved building establishing shots with point-and-click interactions for finding clues, narrative progression, and interactive world-building.
