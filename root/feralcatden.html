Premise

A single-player adventure puzzle noir mystery beyond time, space and in two parallel storylines – a follow up to the award-winning Genesis Noir. Check out the Kickstarter↗ for the project.

Role & Scope

As a 3D environment artist, and prop designer, I contributed to level and cutscene production by modeling, texturing, and set dressing assets that aligned with the game’s distinctive visual style and player UX.

Designing for player exploration

The game narrative has a dense storyline that takes place simultaneously in two alternate universes, leaving a lot of opportunity for the player to explore as much of the world as possible. My role involved building establishing shots with point-and-click interactions for finding clues, narrative progression, and interactive world-building.
